## Day-1 Survival Tips

• **Pretzel Day is Non-Negotiable** – It happens on Fridays (usually). You will hear about it. You will be offered at least three pretzels. Eat them. It's not really about the pretzels; it's about community. Plus, they're warm, salty, and excellent for improv-inspired conversation starters.

• **The Annex Door Sticks** – There's a supply closet and stairwell near the warehouse. The door has accepted its fate. Lift and pull with authority. Dwight (Assistant to the Regional Manager, obsessed with order, drives a Beetle with a tire pump) will be glad you know this. Show him you know this.

• **Bowling League Has an Opening** – We have a company team. You mentioned interest; we're interested in you. Thursday nights at Pin Pals alley. Jim, Pam, and Kevin usually play. Fair warning: Kevin uses bumpers.

• **The Café Disco is Real and Also Possibly Structurally Questionable** – The basement holds mysteries. One of them is a disco. Hours vary. Check with someone who looks like they've actually been there. (Spoiler: Multiple people have. Separately. It's a thing.)

• **Expense Reporting Matters** – Vision insurance forms go through official channels. Do not adopt Kevin's filing philosophy (it involves a lot of hope). Dwight monitors compliance. Channel your sudoku brain here—rules matter, patterns emerge, and Dwight will verify.
